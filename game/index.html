Walk into a coral story marker to start the next job; if a mission fails, press Enter or walk back into the marker to retry. The safehouse on the Strip patches you up and writes the save. Between jobs, collect cash stashes, take a glowing car to the Motor Row chop shop, or step into the red Heat Run ring and survive three stars for forty-two seconds. Click the world to capture the mouse. Guns are hitscan: the crosshair decides, the muzzle flash and tracer show the line, and parked cars, walls and cruisers all stop a round. Armor eats part of every hit; R reloads from reserve, 1, 2 and 3 swap the Solano 9, the Kestrel SMG and the Tidebreaker 12 shotgun. Draw a weapon near a cruiser and the heat comes fast — break line of sight, stay quiet and the stars cool one at a time.
